Yahoo's One-Win standings and each week's matchup odds. The odds simulate every team's lineup over the week's NBA schedule, from player projections: Yahoo's (made by Rotowire), ESPN's, or their average. Before a week locks they use each team's best lineup and change with every update. Once it locks, the odds are live: the games played so far count as played, only the rest is projected, so by the week's end they're the result. Each bar shows them against the odds frozen at the lineup lock (Monday). Team-level only.
